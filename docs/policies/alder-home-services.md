# Alder Home Services — Appointment Promise

*Fictional. Effective 1 September 2026.*

## 2. Missed appointments

We book you a slot and we keep it. If nobody attends within your booked slot we credit $9
against your next visit, and up to $12 where you have waited in for the full slot.

We do not ask you to prove you were at home. If our engineer did not attend, our own records
will show it.
