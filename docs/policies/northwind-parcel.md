# Northwind Parcel — Delivery Guarantee

*Fictional. Effective 1 September 2026.*

## 4. When a delivery goes wrong

**4.1 Late delivery.** We give you a delivery window when you order. If your parcel arrives
outside the window we gave you, we credit $8, or up to $10 where the delay exceeds two
hours.

**4.2 Recorded as delivered, not received.** Where a parcel is recorded as delivered but was
not received, we credit $12. Where the customer provides evidence covering the recorded
delivery time, we credit up to $15.

**4.3 Making a claim.** Claims may be made through your account, by email, or by an agent
acting on your behalf. We do not require a claim to be made in any particular form.
